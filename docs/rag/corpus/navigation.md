# Site navigation

The Home page is at /. Its Next Step link moves to the featured builds on that page. View All Builds opens /builds, the Car Builds index.

View Details opens an individual sample build detail page. The Skyline is at /builds/skyline, the Miata at /builds/miata, and the BMW at /builds/bmw. These pages show the example car and labeled modification categories. To look at the upgrades on one example car, open Car Builds and then View Details.

The About page at /about explains Car Builders Place. The professional portfolio is at /portfolio, the assistant case study at /portfolio/projects/rag, and the Knowledge Assistant at /rag.
