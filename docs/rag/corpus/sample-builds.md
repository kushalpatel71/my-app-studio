# Understanding sample builds

Car Builders Place presents three static sample builds: a Nissan Skyline, Mazda MX-5 Miata, and BMW. They offer inspiration and examples of how modifications can be organized.

The sample descriptions are illustrative concepts. The photographs are representative images, not evidence that the pictured cars have the listed parts. The site does not verify pictured-car specifications, real owner histories, measured horsepower gains, or compatibility with a visitor's car.

A build detail page groups example modifications under wheels, suspension, engine, and performance. These categories help compare ideas; they are not a parts recommendation or repair instruction.
